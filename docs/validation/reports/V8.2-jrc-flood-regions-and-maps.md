Date: 2026-11-17 · crc-sdk 0.11.1, crc-framework 0.6.0 · awaiting review

Stage 2 of the plan of 2026-11-17. The map explorer now has regions (Texas, Fremont, the Rhine) and

## What the review shows about the findings

Your reading was that missing curves and bad values sit where the data should not be used. A probe
against JRC's own permanent-water layer supports that for part of them and not for the rest:

| Flooded pixels, Texas box | Pixels | In JRC's permanent water |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get a curve | 9,853,959 | 1,894,754 (19%) |
| No curve: all depths equal (1 m) | 698,317 | 698,317 |
| No curve: a depth decreases | 192,683 | 192,619 |
| No curve: fewer than four depths | 941,251 | 0 |

- **Equal and decreasing depths are water bodies.** JRC patched its maps with permanent water; 1 m is
  the patch value. Losing these pixels loses nothing about flooding. Same in Houston, Austin and
  Fremont (every such pixel, bar one in Houston).
- **Fewer than four depths is land.** These pixels flood only at the longer return periods and none
  is permanent water: half of the pixels left out in the Texas box, and 44,732 in the Houston box.
  This part of CRC-014-JRC stands as a loss of real flood extent.
- **A fifth of the curves describe a water body.** They are fitted to depths that include the patch
  value and are delivered unmarked (added to CRC-019-JRC). This also explains the county ranking.
- **Curve on dry ground and below zero (CRC-015-JRC)** are for the map to show; I have not
  characterised where they lie beyond the counts.

On the map, in each area: "Review: flooded pixels without a curve, share", "dry in JRC's map, curve
gives 0.1 m or more", "curve below zero", "largest distance of curve from published depth", "pixels
deeper than 10 m" and "... inside JRC's spurious-depth mask", beside JRC's published depth and
crc-sdk's fitted depth. The permanent-water layer itself is not on the map yet.

## Departures from the plan

- Texas counties and the Fremont boundary come from the U.S. Census TIGERweb service (vintage 1
  January 2026), not from geoBoundaries: its county file has no state field.
- The fitted layers were built from the crc-sdk outputs of Stage 1, copied into the explorer's
  cache, not from a second 27-minute run. A clean rebuild would run crc-sdk again.
- Review layers, finding charts, the draft preview and the permanent-water probe were not in the
  plan; they answer your request to review the findings with graphs and maps.
- `site/shared/sources.js` is shared with the hail page. Regions are opt-in and the hail page does
  not opt in; its browser checks pass unchanged.
- `for_assets` (which row an asset gets, plan candidate CRC-022) was not run.

## Limits and loose ends

- The site grew by about 11 MB of committed data. If that is too much for the repository, the
  review layers can be built on demand instead.
- A cell of a fitted layer shows its deepest pixel; in a cell with a river channel that is the
  channel. The legend says so.
- The explorer has not been checked at phone width with the region switcher (the map pages were
  never built for phones).
- The demo video was not re-recorded.
