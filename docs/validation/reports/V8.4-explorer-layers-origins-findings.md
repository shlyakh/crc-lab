# Report V8.4: picking layers, where each layer comes from, findings on the map

Date: 2026-11-17 · crc-sdk 0.11.1, crc-framework 0.6.0 · awaiting review

Stage 4, asked for on 2026-11-17 after V8.1 to V8.3: easier navigation between layers, a clear mark
of what comes from crc-sdk and what was read directly, and the findings shown on the map. Committed
locally on `main`; nothing is pushed. The findings themselves are unchanged and wait for your
review.

## How to look

- Map: `just serve`, then `/validation/explore/`, source "River flood depth (JRC GloFAS, EFAS)".
- Findings from the assessments page: `/validation/`, open a finding; "On the map" has one link per
  area. This works on the real page, since findings are in the committed inventory.
- The draft assessment with its tagged tables and charts: `just validation-draft-preview jrc-flood`.

## 1. Picking a layer

The river flood source has 73 layers. They are now reached through three controls instead of one
long list:

| Control | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Area | Texas box, Houston close-up, Austin close-up (in Fremont and on the Rhine, the one area) |
| Show | Fitted by crc-sdk · Published by JRC · Findings · Outside evidence (FEMA) |
| Layer | The layers left: 1 fitted, 1 or 2 published, 8 or 10 findings, 4 evidence |

- Changing the area keeps the same view where the other area has it.
- "previous" and "next" step through the layers; so do Arrow up and Arrow down when no field or
  list has the focus. A counter says "2 of 10".
- The address carries the layer (`layer=`), so a view can be linked; a link that names only a layer
  opens its source and region.
- Sources with few layers (ERA5, CRC Open) look as before.

## 2. Where a layer comes from

Every layer of the explorer has one of three origins, shown as a coloured tag beside the source
title, in the legend and in the detail panel, and as a small mark per origin in the source list:

| Tag | Meaning | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| crc-sdk output | What crc-sdk returns | Cyclone tracks, water levels, station temperatures, ERA5, CRC Open; river flood "Fitted by crc-sdk" |
| Publisher's files, read by us | Computed by the validation project from the publisher's own files | River flood "Published by JRC" and "Findings" |
| Outside evidence | Other publishers, set beside the source | River flood "Outside evidence (FEMA)"; gauges and Harvey marks |

- River flood carries all three marks in the source list; the other sources one.
- The words "Review:" and "Evidence:" were dropped from layer titles; the tag says it.
- On the assessment page each table and chart of the JRC assessment carries the same tag, and the
  comparisons are tagged as outside evidence.
- The hail page does not show tags (it has its own "experimental" mark); a fourth origin, "Built in
  crc-lab", is defined for it and not switched on.

## 3. Findings on the map

Under Show: Findings the layers are listed by finding. Picking one puts the finding's text
(title, observed, consequence, documented) in the left column.

| Finding | Layers per area | New in this stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRC-014-JRC, flooded pixels without a curve | All causes; land (fewer than four depths); water bodies (equal or decreasing depths) | The split into land and water |
| CRC-013-FWK, fit does not converge | Pixels per cell | New. Totals equal the assessment's: 2,821 Texas box, 82 Houston, 0 Austin, 5 Fremont, 49 Rhine |
| CRC-015-JRC, curve against published depth | Dry in JRC's map with curve of 0.1 m or more; curve below zero; largest distance | None |
| CRC-019-JRC, masks not read | Deeper than 10 m; of those in the spurious-depth mask; flooded pixels that are permanent water | Permanent water, now also for the Texas box |

- On the assessments page these four findings have "On the map" links, one per area. The other
  JRC findings say "Not a finding with a place on the map" (licence, error message, release
  identity, `source_id`, cache path, run time). Findings of other sources say nothing about a map.
- The Rhine has no spurious-depth or permanent-water layer: the EFAS masks were not read.

## 4. Finding your way on the assessments page (added after your first look)

You could not see how to open a finding, and the draft preview looked like the real page. Both
were the same problem: the page is long and its parts were only reachable by scrolling.

- A bar "On this page" under the summary tiles, which stays at the top while scrolling: Inventory,
  each assessment by name, Findings, Not yet assessed.
- A finding is a row in the Findings list; selecting it opens it. The list now says so, and an
  address such as `/validation/#finding-CRC-014-JRC` opens that finding and brings it into view.
- In the inventory, a source's detail links to its assessment and to each of its findings.
- The preview shows a framed note "Draft preview" with a link "Go to the draft"; the real page
  never shows it. The preview's only other difference is the fourth assessment block.
- The explorer's finding panel links to that finding on the assessments page.

## 5. One page (2026-11-17, at your word)
